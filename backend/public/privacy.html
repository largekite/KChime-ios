Privacy Policy

App: KChime
Company: LargeKite Capital Intelligence LLC
Effective Date: March 6, 2026
Contact: privacy@kchime.com

1. Overview

KChime is an AI-powered message reply assistant for iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

  • Message text you enter: Text you type or speak into the app is sent to our server and to the Anthropic AI API solely to generate reply suggestions. This text is not stored by us after processing.
  • Voice/microphone data: If you use the mic button, your speech is transcribed on-device by iOS Speech Recognition (Apple). We receive only the resulting text, not the audio recording.
  • Usage counts: We track how many replies you generate per day (as a number only) to enforce free plan limits. This is tied to an anonymous device ID, not your identity.
  • Apple ID (optional): If you sign in with Apple, we store your Apple user ID and a JWT token to identify your account. We do not receive your name or email unless you choose to share them.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not store the messages you type or the replies generated.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, or any other device data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic API: Message text is sent to Anthropic to generate AI reply suggestions. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to their processing. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train models by default.
  • Supabase: We use Supabase (PostgreSQL) to store anonymous usage counts and account data. Data is stored in the US.
  • Apple Speech Recognition: On-device speech-to-text. Subject to Apple's privacy policy.

5. Microphone & Speech Recognition

KChime requests microphone and speech recognition access only to transcribe messages you choose to dictate. You can deny this permission and still use the app by typing manually. Audio is never recorded or stored — only the transcribed text is used.

6. Data Retention

Anonymous usage logs (reply counts) are retained for 30 days then deleted. If you have an account, your account data is retained until you delete your account via Settings → Delete Account.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time via the app (Settings → Delete Account) or by emailing privacy@kchime.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Children

KChime is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
